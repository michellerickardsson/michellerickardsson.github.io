Ask about tonight

Light pollution levels are estimated by hand for each location (there's no free live API for this). Weather and aurora activity are fetched live from Open-Meteo and NOAA. Moon phase and the darkest time of night are calculated from real astronomical data (sunrise-sunset.org) and a known lunar formula. Moonlight's impact is grouped into rough steps based on illumination — in reality brightness doesn't increase perfectly linearly, especially near full moon. The star score is mapped to the real Bortle dark-sky scale used by astronomers.
