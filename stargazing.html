Stargazing

Where in Stockholm is the sky darkest tonight? This app checks live weather, moon phase and aurora activity, combines it with an estimated light pollution level for a handful of nature areas around the city, and scores each one. Click a marker for the details.

Light pollution levels are estimated by hand for each location (there's no free live API for this), based on distance from central Stockholm. Weather and aurora activity are fetched live from Open-Meteo and NOAA. Moon phase is calculated locally from the date.
