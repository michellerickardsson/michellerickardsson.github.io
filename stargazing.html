Light pollution levels are estimated by hand for each location. Weather and aurora activity are fetched live from Open-Meteo and NOAA. Moon phase and darkness are calculated from real astronomical data (sunrise-sunset.org) and a known lunar formula. Star Score is mapped to the real Bortle dark-sky scale astronomers use. The map preview only applies the aurora bonus to tonight, since solar activity can't be forecast a week ahead.
