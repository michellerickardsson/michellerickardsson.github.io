02

How I work

Swap these steps for your own way of working. This is a sequence, so the numbering earns its place here in a way it would not in the work list.

  1. 1

    Understand before measuring

    Who is the audience and what is stopping them? Numbers without context mislead, so I start by listening and reading before setting metrics.

  2. 2

    Write down a guess

    A hypothesis you can be wrong about. If I cannot say what would disprove it, it is not a hypothesis, just an opinion.

  3. 3

    Test small

    Fast and cheap first. A test that takes three weeks to set up can go stale before it answers anything.

  4. 4

    Keep, kill or scale

    The decision rule is set in advance, not after. Otherwise every result becomes something you rationalise afterwards.
